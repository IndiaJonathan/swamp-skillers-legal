Swamp Skillers privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Swamp Skillers is a chat game I run in the live chat of my YouTube channel, Swampy Songs. It uses YouTube API Services.

What I store

When you type a game command in my live chat, I store your YouTube display name, your YouTube channel ID, the commands you send to the game, and your game progress (skills, levels, items). Nothing else.

Where it is kept and how it is used

This data is kept only on my own computer and is used only to run the game and show your character (for example, a leaderboard or your name over your character on stream). I do not sell or share it with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Your game data is kept while you play. Type !quit and then !quit yes in chat to delete your character and its data, or ask me through my YouTube channel and I will delete it.

YouTube and Google

By playing you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke this app's access at Google security settings.

Contact

Through my YouTube channel: youtube.com/@SwampySongs.
